About the Codex

This is a curated visual reference of 100+ internet memes from around the world, aimed at the millennial generation who lived through them. It is not exhaustive — it's a starting point for understanding what your younger relatives, coworkers, and timeline are referencing.

Each entry includes the origin, meaning, peak year, and where it came from. Sources are cited per-entry, with most data drawn from Know Your Meme, Wikipedia, and major news outlets.

The site is open-source on GitHub. Have a meme that's missing? Open an issue.

Sources

  • Know Your Meme — primary source for image URLs and origin data
  • Wikipedia — cultural context
  • Major news outlets — peak-year verification

Coverage

  • USA / UK (Western internet)
  • Japan, Korea, China (East Asia)
  • France, Germany, Russia, Italy, Spain (Europe)
  • Brazil, Mexico (Latin America)
  • Indonesia, Philippines, Vietnam (SEA)

Era breakdown

  • 2007–2012 — Classic Web (rage comics, LOLcats, advice animals)
  • 2012–2017 — Peak (Doge, Pepe, image macros go mainstream)
  • 2017–2020 — Post-irony (NPC, OK Boomer, dank Maymay)
  • 2020–2026 — Gen Z era (brainrot, Skibidi, poly-formats)
